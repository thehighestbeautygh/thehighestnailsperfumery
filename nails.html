Nail Care: Myth or Fact?

Open the cards below.

Myth or Fact: Nails need to “breathe” between sets.

Healthy natural nails do not breathe through the air. Proper application, removal and general nail care are important.

Myth or Fact: Picking off a nail set is a good way to remove it.

Picking or peeling can damage the natural nail. Proper removal is the safer option.
