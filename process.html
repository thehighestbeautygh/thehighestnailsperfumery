AFTER YOUR APPOINTMENT

Keep the Highest finish.

A little care after your appointment can help your nails, lashes and fragrance experience stay beautiful for longer.

01 NAILS

Take care of the details.

  • Moisturize your hands and cuticles regularly.
  • Avoid using your nails as tools.
  • Wear gloves when doing prolonged cleaning or working with harsh products.
  • Avoid picking, peeling or pulling at your nails or enhancements.
  • Book your maintenance or refill when needed.
02 LASHES

Let your lashes stay effortless.

  • Avoid rubbing or pulling around the eye area.
  • Clean your lashes gently and regularly.
  • Be mindful of excessive heat or steam, especially around a fresh set.
  • Never pick or remove extensions yourself.
  • Follow your lash technician's guidance for refills and maintenance.
03 PERFUMERY

Make your scent last.

  • Apply fragrance to moisturized skin to help the scent last longer.
  • Focus on pulse points such as the wrists and neck.
  • Avoid aggressively rubbing fragrance into the skin.
  • A light mist on clothing can add another layer of scent, depending on the fabric.
  • Store your fragrance away from direct sunlight and excessive heat.
